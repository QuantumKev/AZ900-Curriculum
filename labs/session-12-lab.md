# Session 12 lab — Exam conditions and the sandbox

**Time:** 45 minutes for the set, plus the debrief in the instructor notes
**Objectives practiced:** all 57
**You need:** the final readiness set, a pen, a timer the whole room can see. No subscription.

This lab is the timed sitting. There is no portal work.

---

## Part A — The set (45 minutes)

1. Put the objective checklist and all notes on the floor or in a bag. Not on the desk.
2. Phones face down, silent.
3. Open [final-readiness-set.md](../quizzes/final-readiness-set.md) or the printed handout. The handout is questions only.
4. The instructor starts the clock. Stop at 45 minutes, even if you are not done.
5. Guess rather than leave an item blank. Bring the paper in when time is called.

You already took the official practice assessment before this session, or you will take it on day 7 of the post-course plan. Do not open it during these 45 minutes. Two instruments, two sittings.

### No-cost path

This is the no-cost path. The readiness set is in the repository. The official assessment requires only a Microsoft Learn account:
[AZ-900 practice assessment](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/credentials/certifications/exams/az-900/practice/assessment?assessment-type=practice&assessmentId=23).

## Part B — Log misses (during the debrief, not during the 45)

For every item you missed, write the objective ID on the checklist and mark that objective shaky. If you do not know what the objective was asking, mark it blank.

Compute three numbers: overall percent, and a percent for each domain if the handout's answer grid supports it. Compare them to the bar: 85 percent overall, no domain under 75 percent.

## Part C — Sandbox (10 minutes, instructor-led)

Open [aka.ms/examdemo](https://aka.ms/examdemo). Click through far enough to see how an item is marked for review and how you move next and back. Do not treat the sandbox items as AZ-900 content.

## Instructor key

The answer key is the second half of [final-readiness-set.md](../quizzes/final-readiness-set.md). Do not display it during the 45 minutes.

Score by domain before you discuss items. Choose three objective IDs with the most misses for the clinic. Record cohort percents in the readiness tracker the same day, while the papers are still in the room.
