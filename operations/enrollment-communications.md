# Enrollment communications

Copy, fill the brackets, send. Do not add a calendar date to the curriculum files; put the meeting time only in the message you send.

Brackets you will replace:

- `[First name]`
- `[meeting time]`
- `[room or link]`
- `[instructor name]`
- `[how they get the syllabus]` — link, attachment, or printed copy

Tone is plain. These learners are adult beginners. Do not tell them the course will be easy.

Before that meeting:

1. Create a free Microsoft Learn account with a personal Microsoft account, and sign in once at https://learn.microsoft.com so the first module is not blocked by a signup.
2. Read the syllabus: [how they get the syllabus]. It is the contract for how the course runs, including how quizzes work.

Check the current offer yourself at https://azure.microsoft.com/en-us/free/ before you enter a payment method. If you would rather not, say so. Every lab has a no-cost way through.

What this certification is: vocabulary and judgment about cloud and Azure. What it is not: a job interview, and not an exam where Microsoft Learn is open on a second monitor. Fundamentals exams keep Learn closed. We will practice that way.

Reply with the name you want on the roster, and whether you plan to use a free Azure account, an account we provide, or the no-cost path.

Subject: What we did, and what Session 2 actually teaches

Hello [First name],

Today was introductions and a practice assessment. That assessment previews the whole exam. It was supposed to feel broad. It was not a grade, and it was not the Session 1 lesson.

Session 2 is [meeting time], [room or link]. Bring the objective checklist if you were given one. If you were not, you will get it at the door.

If you want a head start, complete this module, including its short assessment: https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/training/modules/describe-cloud-compute/

The score you saw today does not say whether you will pass in six weeks. It says which words are still unfamiliar. That is a normal first day.
