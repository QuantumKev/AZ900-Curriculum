# Exam scheduling

What to say in Session 12, and what a learner does on day 1 of the ten-day window. Facts below are the ones the course overview already traced to Microsoft Learn. If a policy page disagrees on the day you teach, the page wins, and you update this sheet before the next cohort.

## Who schedules

The learner schedules. You do not hold their Pearson VUE password and you do not sit in their cart. Your job on day 1 is to make sure an appointment exists, or a written reason it does not (accommodations still pending, voucher not issued, they are rescheduling).

Put the status in the tracker column `exam_status`: `not_scheduled`, `scheduled`, `rescheduled`, `passed`, or `failed`.

## Day 1 steps for the learner

1. Sign in at Microsoft Learn with the same account they have been using for modules.
2. Open the [AZ-900 exam page](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/credentials/certifications/exams/az-900/) and start scheduling.
3. If they need accommodations, stop and use the accommodations request first. Do not book and then ask.
4. Choose a seat on day 8, 9, or 10 of the window. Online proctored or test center is their choice; the rules of the sitting are the same either way.
5. If a school or employer voucher is required, use it in this step. A voucher does not change the readiness bar.
6. Send the instructor the appointment day (day 8, 9, or 10) and whether it is online or a test center. Not the home address.

## Students and educators

Certiport is the channel named for students and educators. If this cohort is on a school voucher, confirm with the program lead which portal is actually funded before Session 12. Telling a class "just use Pearson VUE" when their voucher is Certiport wastes the week. Write the confirmed portal in the session log.

## If they are not ready on day 9

Reschedule. The bar is 85 percent overall, no domain under 75 percent, on the official practice assessment, and a checklist with no blanks.

Say this: a first failed attempt costs a fee and imposes a 24-hour wait; further failures wait longer. An unused appointment can be moved. Read the current retake page before you quote anything past the 24-hour first retake, because the later waits are easy to misremember.

## After they pass or fail

Passed: set `exam_status` to `passed`. Fundamentals does not expire, so do not sell them a renewal calendar.

Failed: set `failed`, note the domain scores if the score report gives them, and point at the retake policy. Do not immediately book the next morning unless 24 hours have passed and the miss was narrow. A broad miss means they repair the blank objectives first.

## What not to do

- Do not provide exam questions, dumps, or "what I saw on my exam."
- Do not ask learners to photograph the exam screen.
- Do not guarantee that a practice score will produce a 700.
