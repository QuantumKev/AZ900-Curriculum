# Readiness tracker

The spreadsheet is [`readiness-tracker.csv`](readiness-tracker.csv). One row per learner. Percents are whole numbers. Leave a cell blank if they were absent; do not write zero unless they sat and scored zero.

## Columns

| Column | What goes in it |
| --- | --- |
| learner | Name as they want it on the roster |
| hands_on_path | `free`, `sponsored`, or `nocost` |
| learn_account | `yes` once they have signed in to Microsoft Learn |
| s01–s11 | Session quiz percent |
| d1_checkpoint | Domain 1 checkpoint at the start of Session 3 |
| d2_checkpoint | Domain 2 checkpoint at the start of Session 8 |
| s11_as_d3 | Session 11 quiz percent. That quiz is the Domain 3 checkpoint |
| final_overall, final_d1, final_d2, final_d3 | Session 12 readiness set |
| official_1 | Official practice assessment taken before Session 12 |
| official_2 | Official practice assessment taken on day 7 or day 9 of the window |
| checklist_blanks | Count of objectives still marked blank after Session 12 |
| exam_status | `not_scheduled`, `scheduled`, `rescheduled`, `passed`, `failed` |
| notes | Objective IDs to repair. Not a narrative |

## How to use the numbers

Session quizzes: target 80 percent. Under that, look at which items. Two shared misses in the room get a one-sentence reteach. One learner's miss gets a module unit, not a second lecture.

Checkpoints: record them the same day. The two weakest objective IDs from the Domain 1 checkpoint feed Session 4 and 5 warm-ups. The two weakest from Domain 2 feed the Session 11 clinic. Write those IDs in the session log, not only in your head.

Session 12 bar, from the course overview:

- final overall at least 85
- final_d1, final_d2, and final_d3 each at least 75
- official_2 meets the same bar
- checklist_blanks is 0

Both instruments have to clear. A good course-set score does not replace the official assessment, and the reverse is also true.

## When someone is behind

| What you see | What you do |
| --- | --- |
| Absent | Send the missed-session message. Blank cell, not a zero |
| Under 80 on one quiz, fine on the next | Note the objective IDs. Do not intervene unless the same ID appears again |
| Same objective ID missed twice | Assign that unit. Check it at the next warm-up |
| Domain checkpoint under 60 | They do that domain's learning path assessments before the next session, and you look at their paper yourself |
| Day 9 official attempt under the bar | Tell them to reschedule. Do not coach them into the seat |
| No Learn account by Session 2 | They cannot do the assignment. Fix that before discussing scores |

Do not enter payment status, disability detail, or accommodation medical information in this sheet. Accommodation status belongs in a private note, and the exam-scheduling page says when to request it.
