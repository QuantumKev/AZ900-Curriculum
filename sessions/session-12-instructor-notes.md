**Domain minutes:** Domain 1 — 50, Domain 2 — 25, Domain 3 — 45

## Before you walk in

- Readiness set printed, or the form timed. Answer key not visible.
- Official practice scores from Session 11 homework collected as they walk in. A learner who did not take it still sits the course set today and takes the official one on day 7 of the plan.
- Sandbox link tested on the room network: [aka.ms/examdemo](https://aka.ms/examdemo).
- Exam-scheduling one-pager from [`../operations/exam-scheduling.md`](../operations/exam-scheduling.md) available to hand out. It has no date on it. They write their own.
- Objective checklists in the room. They will log misses on them.

## Timed block

Read the rules from slide 2 once. Start the clock in public. Do not answer content questions during the 45. If someone asks what a word means, say "treat it as you will on the exam." That feels harsh and is the point of the sitting.

45 minutes matches Fundamentals exam time, not seat time. Tell them the difference after the timer, not before, or they will think they have 65 minutes of questions.

## Debrief

Score by domain first. The set is built to be weight-aligned; a healthy total can hide a failing domain. The readiness bar is 85 percent overall and no domain under 75 percent, on this set and on a separate official attempt, plus no blank objectives.

Then item review only for items the room missed. Correct reasoning, objective ID, one source. Log the ID on the checklist. Do not reread items everyone got.

Put the three weakest objective IDs on the board. That is the clinic. If you cannot tell, pick the three with the most misses, and break ties in favor of Domain 1.

## Gap clinic

Five to eight minutes each. Definition, one scenario, one contrast they confused. Stop. This is not Session 2 again.

## Logistics — say only what the course overview already verified

- 45 minutes exam, 65 minutes seat.
- 700 or greater.
- Proctored. Pearson VUE, or Certiport for students and educators.
- Learn is not available.
- First retake wait is 24 hours; later retakes wait longer. Read the current policy page before you add any tighter number. If the page has changed, the page wins.
- Fundamentals certifications do not expire.
- Accommodations before registration.
- Question count is not published as an AZ-900-specific number. Do not invent "you will see 40 questions." The overview marks that as **NEEDS VERIFICATION**.

Open the sandbox. Show next, a review flag if it is there, and submit. Five minutes. Do not take a second practice exam inside the sandbox.

## 10-day plan

Walk the table on the slide, which matches the course overview. The operational version, including what you do when someone is under the bar, is in [`../operations/exam-scheduling.md`](../operations/exam-scheduling.md).

The sentence to send them out with: schedule on day 1 of the window, for day 8, 9, or 10. If day 9's practice misses the bar, move the appointment.

## If the room started late

Shorten the sandbox before you shorten the timed set. Shorten logistics talk before you shorten the clinic. The 45 minutes is the session.
