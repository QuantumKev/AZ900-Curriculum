# Session 12 slides — Exam Readiness

Each `##` heading is one slide. Notes: [session-12-instructor-notes.md](session-12-instructor-notes.md).

## 1. Exam day practice

Session 12. No new objectives. 45 minutes under exam rules, then we repair what missed.

## 2. Rules for the next 45 minutes

- 45 minutes. Stop when time is called.
- No notes, no checklist, no neighbor.
- No Microsoft Learn. Fundamentals exams do not include it, so this sitting does not either.
- Mark a guess and move. Blank items score the same as wrong; there is no penalty stated for guessing, and a blank teaches you less in the review.

We are using the course readiness set in class. You already took Microsoft's practice assessment as homework. Two different instruments, on purpose.

## 3. After the timer

Score by domain, not only by total.

Every miss gets an objective ID on your checklist. That ID is the homework, not "study networking."

## 4. The readiness bar

85 percent or better overall, and no domain under 75 percent, on this set and on a separate official practice assessment.

No objective still marked blank.

Microsoft does not publish a conversion from practice scores to the exam. The bar sits above 700 on purpose. If you are under it on day 9 of the window, reschedule. Do not sit and hope.

## 5. What the real exam will feel like

Exam time: 45 minutes. Seat time: 65 minutes. The extra time is check-in and agreements, not extra questions.

Passing score: 700 or greater.

Proctored. Schedule with Pearson VUE, or with Certiport if you are going through a student or educator channel.

Microsoft Learn stays closed.

Fundamentals certifications do not expire.

## 6. If you do not pass

A first retake is allowed 24 hours after the failed attempt. Later retakes wait longer.

A reschedule before you sit costs less than a fail. We would rather move the appointment on day 9 than use the retake policy on day 10.
